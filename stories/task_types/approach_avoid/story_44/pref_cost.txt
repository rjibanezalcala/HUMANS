1) Admission for each visitor is expensive in comparison to other museums.
2) Parking is metered and you will only be allowed a maximum of an hour and a half in the museum.
3) No food or drinks, not even water, are allowed in the exhibit spaces.
4) There are no benches inside the museum, so you will not be able to sit for the entire visit.
5) The only section where you are allowed to take photos is the modern art section.
6) Your bag will be checked at the entrance.
