1) You have to photograph the item you are trying to sell from 5 different angles each time you want to sell an item.
2) The description of each item has to be at least 4 sentences and also include measurements.
3) You have to learn how to work and navigate the confusing clothing resale app.
4) You need to purchase packaging materials for shipping the clothes to customers.
5) You have to pay all the shipping fees; they can't be charged to the customer.
6) You owe 10% of the sales price of each item to the clothing resale app.
