Instructions (Section 1 of 5)

In this first section, you will evaluate a series of individual items or activities presented one at a time. For each item, indicate your personal preference using the slider provided, ranging from 0 (not at all) to 100 (a lot).

0, or "not at all," represents a catastrophic or miserable experience, the most distressing, miserable, unbearable situation you could face.

100, or "a lot," represents an extraordinary, ideal, perfect experience. The most desirable or rewarding scenario you could realistically imagine.

Most everyday items, experiences, or minor inconveniences will fall somewhere between these two extremes. There are no right or wrong answers. Do not overthink your responses, simply rely on your immediate gut reaction and assign the number that best reflects how appealing or unappealing each item is to you personally right now. Keep in mind that you must move the slider even just to return back to neutral if that is your preference and wait five seconds to move on to the next question.
