Please read the following overview before beginning!

Please do not close this tab or this browser, or your progress may be lost.

This questionnaire is divided into 5 sections. You will complete them sequentially in a single session estimated to take around 50 minutes to complete.

  1. Initial item ratings. In this section you will evaluate individual items and scenarios one at a time on a scale from 0 (extremely undesirable) to 100 (incredibly desirable).
  2. You will evaluate some more individual items on the same 0 to 100 scale.
  3. Combined item ratings. You will rate pairs of items presented together, indicating how desirable having both items simultaneously would be on the same 0 to 100 scale.
  4. Multiple-choice section. You will answer questions where you choose your single most preferred option from sets of 2, 3, or 4 choices.
  5. Final item ratings. You will complete a final re-rating of individual items on the 0 to 100 scale.

While completing the questionnaire, each question will have a five second delay before you can respond. For questions with a slider, you must move the slider (even if it’s just a little and return it to the central position) before moving on to the next question. In the multiple-choice section, an answer must be selected to advance to the next question.

Instructions will be provided at the beginning of each section.
